**Project:** Forget-lah - Intelligent patient follow-up. Staff remain in control.

**Problem statement:** Patient Follow-up

Dental clinics maintain regular follow-up schedules for routine examinations, preventive care and ongoing treatments. Staff manually review records and contact patients individually. As the patient database grows, consistent follow-up becomes harder, with the risk of missed appointments and delayed treatment. Forget-lah addresses the coordination work after the reminder while retaining clinic instructions, source ownership and staff judgment.
